Guidelines & Privacy Policy

How to use NPK Advisor responsibly and how we handle your data.

📋 Usage Guidelines
  1. Always obtain your NPK values from a certified soil testing laboratory before using this tool. Informal or self-tested values may produce inaccurate results.
  2. Ensure all NPK values are entered in kg/ha (kilograms per hectare). Entering values in any other unit without conversion will lead to incorrect recommendations.
  3. Select the crop type that most closely matches what you are currently growing or planning to grow. Crop selection directly affects the threshold ranges used in the analysis.
  4. Collect soil samples from multiple spots across your field and combine them into a single composite sample before sending for lab testing, for a more representative result.
  5. Enter your soil pH value when available. It helps identify nutrient availability issues that raw NPK numbers alone may not reveal.
  6. This tool covers Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium only. If your crops are underperforming despite optimal NPK levels, consult an expert regarding secondary nutrients and micronutrients.
  7. Recommendations generated by this system are based on general agricultural guidelines and may not reflect region-specific or variety-specific requirements.
  8. This tool is an assistant, not a replacement for professional expertise. Always consult a qualified agronomist or agricultural officer before applying fertilizers based on these results.
  9. Expert opinion must be sought and considered before acting on any calculated recommendation. Field conditions, local climate, soil texture, and farming history can significantly alter what is actually required.
  10. Do not apply fertilizers at full field scale based solely on this output. Begin with a small trial area and observe crop response before scaling up.
  11. Repeat soil testing at the start of each growing season. Nutrient levels change with rainfall, irrigation, and previous crop uptake — a single test is not permanently valid.
  12. The developers of this system accept no liability for any crop loss, soil damage, or financial impact arising from the use of recommendations produced by this tool.
🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

  1. NPK Advisor does not collect, store, or retain any personal information. No account creation or login is required to use this tool.
  2. All data you enter — including field name, crop type, NPK values, area, and pH — is processed entirely within your browser and is never transmitted to any server.
  3. Your soil data is used solely to compute and display a recommendation on your screen during your active session. It serves no other purpose.
  4. When you close or refresh the page, all entered data is permanently cleared. Nothing is saved between sessions.
  5. Your data is never shared with, sold to, or made accessible to any third party — including advertisers, research organisations, analytics services, or government bodies.
  6. NPK Advisor does not use cookies, browser local storage, session storage, or any form of persistent data mechanism.
  7. No tracking, analytics, fingerprinting, or user behaviour monitoring of any kind is implemented in this application.
  8. This application makes no outbound network requests during use. All computation happens locally on your device — your data never leaves your browser.
  9. NPK Advisor does not display advertisements and is not affiliated with any fertilizer brand, agrochemical company, or commercial agricultural service.
  10. This is a student-developed academic project built for educational purposes. It is not a commercial product and does not operate under a commercial data policy.
